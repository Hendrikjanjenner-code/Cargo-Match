---
name: Deepsea feasibility provenance
description: Why manual laycan arithmetic must not be promoted to route-derived or verified feasibility.
---

Keep manual estimates, licensed route-derived calculations, and independent
operational verification as separate provenance levels. Computing dates from
operator estimates does not promote those estimates to route-derived results.
Confirmed availability and mutual consent do not verify a sea route.

**Why:** The authorized fallback is a manual, provisional pilot when licensed
access is unavailable. A stronger label based only on arithmetic or user
confidence would imply evidence the product does not have.

**How to apply:** When connecting a route provider later, approve the external
subscription and reuse rights separately. A provider calculation can establish
calculated provenance, but verification requires separate evidence tied to the
specific vessel, positioning leg, departure assumptions and laycan. Unknown
travel time, uncertainty or buffer must not silently become zero.
