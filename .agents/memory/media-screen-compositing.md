---
name: Generated laptop-shot geometry
description: Why continuous screen-replacement shots need a finished proof before batching.
---

Treat requested camera framing and settling time as creative guidance, not measured output specifications.

**Why:** Several photorealistic laptop clips requested a display filling roughly three quarters of the frame and an early stationary close-up. The generated clips kept usable screen planes, but delivered smaller displays and later approaches. A prompt alone therefore did not establish readable screen time.

**How to apply:** Finish one moving composite before commissioning the other required shots. Inspect actual corner visibility and screen size throughout the clip, and reserve enough timeline for the measured close-up. Reframe the whole moving shot when useful; a zoom on an isolated screenshot is not a substitute for the physical person-to-laptop camera move. Preserve successful source footage when only the compositor needs fixing.
