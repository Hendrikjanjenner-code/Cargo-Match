---
name: Road operational authority boundary
description: Why the manual road pilot separates driver evidence from commercial consent.
---

Keep operational confirmation and commercial authorization independent, even
when an assigned driver knows the vehicle's actual status better than a planner.

**Why:** The requested pilot deliberately starts with uncertain manual
availability. A trustworthy GPS observation or driver confirmation can improve
operational knowledge without authorizing disclosure, publishing available
capacity, or accepting an introduction. Reusing company membership alone for
all actions would silently erase this separation.

**How to apply:** When adding telemetry, collaboration roles, or a driver app,
review reads as well as writes and notifications. Recheck commercial authority
at consent time; never promote operational evidence into a planner decision.
