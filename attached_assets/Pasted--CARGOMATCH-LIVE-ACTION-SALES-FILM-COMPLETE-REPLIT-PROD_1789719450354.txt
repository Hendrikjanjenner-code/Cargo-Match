# 8. CENTRAL STORY

The film is NOT primarily a product-feature tour.

Tell one clear commercial story.

The problem:

Transport assets complete jobs around the world.

Their next cargo is not always known.

As a result:

vessels reposition in ballast

trucks drive empty kilometres

rail capacity remains unused

aircraft depart with unused cargo capacity.

This costs:

time

fuel

money

capacity utilization.

The opportunity:

The next suitable cargo may already exist nearby.

The problem is that the relevant parties do not always know about each other early enough.

CargoMatch connects:

EXPECTED AVAILABLE CAPACITY

with:

SUITABLE CARGO

before unnecessary empty repositioning occurs.
