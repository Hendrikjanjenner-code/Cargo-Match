CARGOMATCH — DEEPSEA, LINER & DIGITAL IMPORT MASTER EXTENSION V2

Extend the existing CargoMatch application and Master Specification.

The original application may currently be oriented toward inland-waterway vessels such as barges/coasters.

Do NOT remove that functionality.

Expand the architecture so CargoMatch can ultimately support deepsea shipping while preserving the existing secure matching, privacy, company, messaging and network architecture.

The primary new maritime segments are:

1. INLAND WATERWAY
2. DEEPSEA DRY BULK
3. CONVENTIONAL / BREAKBULK
4. LINER SHIPPING
5. LCL / GROUPAGE IMPORT

Do not implement all segments as one identical workflow.

Use shared CargoMatch infrastructure with mode-specific commercial logic.

---

1. DEEPSEA CORE PROBLEM

CargoMatch must solve future vessel-positioning inefficiency.

Example:

An owner has fixed a vessel on a voyage.

The vessel will eventually discharge and become commercially open in Southeast Asia.

The next known employment may require substantial ballast repositioning.

Meanwhile a charterer may have suitable cargo near the vessel's future open position.

The parties may not know about each other.

CargoMatch should identify this opportunity BEFORE the vessel becomes empty.

Core flow:

CURRENT VOYAGE

→ EXPECTED DISCHARGE

→ FUTURE OPEN POSITION

→ FUTURE CARGO

→ ELIGIBILITY

→ MATCH

→ INTEREST

→ CONNECTED

→ COMMERCIAL DISCUSSION

The objective is to create commercial introductions before unnecessary ballast repositioning occurs.

9. BALLAST POSITIONING

Eventually support maritime routing.

Until appropriate routing exists:

label geographic calculations as approximate.

Never describe straight-line distance as actual nautical sailing distance.

Do not claim verified bunker, monetary or emissions savings without a defensible calculation methodology.

10. TWO-SIDED DISCOVERY

CargoMatch must work from both directions.

CHARTERER:

"I have 75,000 mt loading Indonesia."

↓

"What participating vessels are expected to open nearby?"

OWNER:

"My Kamsarmax opens Malaysia 20 October."

↓

"What suitable cargo may be available nearby?"

Both workflows are equally important.
